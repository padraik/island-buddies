## GLOSSARY

- **Range percentile:** where the stock sits between its 52-week low (0%) and high (100%). Low percentile = near the bottom of its yearly range.
- **Breakeven:** the stock price at which the call option's value equals what was paid for it (strike + premium).
- **AM/PM (earnings timing):** whether the company reports before the market opens (AM) or after it closes (PM) — determines which session prices in the reaction.
- **Verified (earnings date):** whether the report date came from the company itself vs. an analyst estimate of cadence.
- **Rule 3 (ratings):** the fund's check that no analyst currently rates the stock Sell, and that the lowest Buy-rated price target still clears the option's breakeven.
- **Rule 6 (reachability):** the fund's check that the move needed to breakeven is realistic against the stock's own historical earnings-day moves (capped at 1.5x the average).
- **Short interest:** the percentage of a stock's tradable shares currently sold short — high short interest means a lot of bearish bets are outstanding, which can amplify a rally if those bets get covered.
- **Open interest (OI):** the number of outstanding contracts for a given option — a rough proxy for how liquid/tradeable it is.
- **IV ramp:** the rise in an option's price purely from rising implied volatility as an earnings date approaches, independent of the stock actually moving yet.
- **Sell the ramp:** exiting a position before the catalyst to capture the IV ramp, rather than holding through the actual print (which risks IV crush even on a correct thesis).
