## GLOSSARY

- **Range percentile:** where the stock sits between its 52-week low (0%) and high (100%). Low percentile = near the bottom of its yearly range.
- **Breakeven:** the stock price at which the call option's value equals what was paid for it (strike + premium).
- **AM/PM (earnings timing):** whether the company reports before the market opens (AM) or after it closes (PM).
- **Verified (earnings date):** whether the report date came from the company itself vs. an analyst estimate of cadence.
- **Rule 3 (ratings):** the fund's check that no analyst currently rates the stock Sell, and that the lowest Buy-rated price target still clears the option's breakeven.
- **Rule 4 (Bear Floor):** the lowest confirmed Buy-rated analyst target must sit above the option's breakeven; if it drops below after entry, exit same day.
- **Rule 6 (reachability):** the fund's check that the move needed to reach breakeven is realistic against the stock's own historical earnings-day moves, capped at 1.5x the *median* (not mean) of the last 4–8 real prints.
- **Decline category:** whether a stock's drop reflects a temporary overreaction (market mispricing something unrelated to the business) or real fundamental/legal deterioration. The fund is built to buy the former, not the latter.
- **Short interest:** the percentage of a stock's tradable shares currently sold short.
- **Open interest (OI):** the number of outstanding contracts for a given option — a rough proxy for liquidity.
- **IV ramp:** the rise in an option's price purely from rising implied volatility as an earnings date approaches.
- **Sell the ramp:** exiting a position before the catalyst to capture the IV ramp, rather than holding through the print itself.
