# Research: SPCE (Virgin Galactic Holdings) -- Aug 11, 2026

**Verdict: KILL. Decline category (Category 2), not a Rule 6 failure.**

## Setup

- Price: ~$3.55 (Aug 11, 2026)
- Percentile: bottom quartile of 52-week range
- Confirmed catalyst: Q2 FY2026 earnings, Aug 12, 2026, PM, `get_earnings_results` verified: true

## Why this looked good on the surface

Best Rule 6 math of the entire fourth batch: needed +5.4% against a 15.30% cap (35% used) at the $3.00 strike, real OTM structure, real 4-quarter sample. Earnings tomorrow, tight but genuine buffer at the Aug 21 expiry.

## Why it's dead anyway

**Ratings already argued against a clean dislocation before the deeper research even started.** Consensus is explicitly Hold (7 analysts), not Buy. Two named firms are actively cutting right now, not holding steady: Goldman Sachs cut twice this year ($47 to $41 to $36), Susquehanna cut ($4 to $3.25). Jefferies is still Buy-rated at $8, a real cushion on paper, but a lone Buy inside a field of active cuts is the same shape as CCL, NKE, and BSX before they cost the fund money.

**The deeper research confirms it's a real business problem, not sentiment.** Q1 2026 revenue was $227,000 against a $65 million net loss. The company has roughly **2.5 quarters of cash runway** at the current burn rate -- a real, near-term liquidity question, not a distant one. Commercial spaceflight, the entire revenue thesis, has been delayed repeatedly; the first spaceplane test flight itself was pushed to Fall 2026, with commercial launch guided for Q4. This is a company that has slipped its own timeline before and is now doing it again, on a shrinking cash clock.

**Decline category: Category 2.** This is not the market punishing a temporary miss on a fundamentally sound business. It's the market correctly pricing real execution risk and a real liquidity question, with the ratings picture (Hold consensus, active cuts) agreeing rather than contradicting that read.

## The lesson worth carrying

Same shape as BTGO earlier tonight and TME back on Aug 8: the best Rule 6 math in the batch, killed anyway on a decline-category read the raw math can't see. Rule 6 answers "can the catalyst move it enough." It does not answer "is there a real reason it's down." Both have to clear.

## GLOSSARY

- **Decline category (Category 1 vs 2):** whether a stock's drop reflects a one-off market overreaction (tradeable) or real, ongoing business deterioration (not tradeable).
- **`verified` flag:** on `get_earnings_results`, true means the date is company-announced; false means it is estimated from reporting cadence.
