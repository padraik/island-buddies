## GLOSSARY

- **6-K:** the form foreign companies use to send the SEC whatever they announce at home. NIO's home exchange is Hong Kong.
- **Ex. 99.1:** the exhibit that carries the actual announcement.
- **Floor gate:** the lowest Buy target only counts as a Rule 4 floor if it was published after the stock's decline.
- **Ramp line:** the last chance to sell before the print, into the pre-earnings premium.
