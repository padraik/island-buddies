## GLOSSARY

- **8-K:** a filing companies must make within a few days of a material event.
- **Item 1.01:** the company entered a material contract.
- **Item 5.02:** a director or officer is leaving or being appointed.
- **Item 7.01 / Regulation FD:** a public disclosure, so no investor hears it first.
- **Item 2.02:** earnings results.
- **EX-99:** an exhibit attached to the filing, usually the press release.
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print, into the pre-earnings premium (binder Tab 4).
- **Dated floor (Tab 6):** a lowest-Buy target only counts for Rule 4 if it's under 60 days old and was published after the stock's decline.
- **Bearxter:** the Baxter whose job is to argue against the trade.
