## GLOSSARY

- **eVTOL:** electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft, an air taxi.
- **R1-R7:** binder Tab 1 Iron Rules. R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 bear floor, R5 price cap on reserve, R6 reachability (needed move <= 1.5x median earnings move), R7 liquidity (OI >= 250, spread <= 25% of mid).
- **BE:** breakeven at expiry, strike + premium.
- **ITM / OTM:** in or out of the money, whether the stock is over the call's strike.
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor counts only if dated inside 60 days and published after the decline.
- **Ramp sell:** selling into the pre-earnings premium instead of holding through the print.
- **GTC:** good till canceled.
- **Dilution:** new shares issued, shrinking each holder's slice.
- **HSR:** Hart-Scott-Rodino, the US antitrust waiting period for deals.
- **25-NSE:** an exchange's notice removing a security from listing.
- **Delta / theta / IV:** option price change per $1 of stock / per day / the volatility priced in.
