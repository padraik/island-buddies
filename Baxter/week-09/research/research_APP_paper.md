## GLOSSARY

- **Paper position:** a trade written down at a real quote with no money.
- **Range percentile (R1):** where the stock sits between its 52-week low (0) and high (1).
- **Breakeven (BE):** strike + premium ($290 + $27.40 = $317.40).
- **Bear floor / Rule 4:** lowest Buy-rated target above breakeven, dated within 60 days and after the decline.
- **Rule 6 (reachability):** move to breakeven no more than 1.5x the median earnings-day move over the last six prints.
- **Rule 7 (liquidity):** OI >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, with one-off items removed; ad-tech's headline profit number.
- **Downgrade cascade:** analysts cutting ratings or targets one after another after a bad print.
- **Category 2 (decline):** the stock is down because the business is actually losing ground, not because the market overreacted (TME, Aug 8).
- **Theta:** value lost per day to time.
- **IV:** implied volatility; rises into earnings, collapses after.
- **Sell the ramp:** sell before the print into the pre-earnings premium.
- **PM print / verified:** results after the close / the company announced the date itself.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Bullxter (for), Calxter (math), Bearxter (against), Macxter (context), Prime (decision).
