## GLOSSARY

- **R1 / Rule 1:** stock in the bottom 20-25% of its 52-week range.
- **R2 / Rule 2 (corrected Oct 8, 11:45am):** expiry at least 21 days after the later of the tool's date and last year's same-quarter date, and the 10-Q deadline at least 5 trading days before expiry; 6-K filers need both dates. "(f)" = `verified: false`.
- **R3 / Rule 3:** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds (shown as Buy/Hold/Sell, e.g. 11/0/0).
- **R4 / Rule 4:** lowest Buy target above breakeven, dated within 60 days and set after the decline (binder Tab 6).
- **R5 / Rule 5:** one contract's ask must fit the conviction tier's budget: $0.676 per share for 3.5/5 at a $676.28 reserve.
- **R6 / Rule 6:** the move to breakeven must be no more than 1.5x the median absolute earnings-day move over recent prints (AM prints: prior close to print-day close).
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid.
- **OI (open interest):** contracts outstanding on that strike.
- **Floor-blocked:** passes on price, but the lowest analyst target hasn't been dated, so Rule 4 can't be signed off.
- **Interpolated bars:** price-history rows the data source filled in where it had no real trades; they can't be used to measure an earnings move.
- **Class action:** a shareholder lawsuit; law firms file these routinely after any big one-day drop.
