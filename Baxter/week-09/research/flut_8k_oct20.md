## The rest (summarized)

- Cuts land in Leeds and Dublin, plus UK&I staff at the Porto and Cluj-Napoca hubs. Flutter has 28,500 employees, 9,000 in UK&I.
- Background in the footnotes: UK iGaming tax went to 40% in April 2026; sports betting (ex-horseracing) goes to 25% in April 2027. 2027 adjusted EBITDA hit from the tax changes: $540M, with $201M of first-order mitigation expected. Restructuring/transaction/integration cost guidance was raised to $500M at Q2.

## GLOSSARY

- **8-K:** a filing companies must make within a few days of a material event.
- **Item 2.05:** the 8-K item for a layoff or exit plan with material costs.
- **Item 7.01 / Regulation FD:** a public disclosure so no investor hears it first.
- **EX-99.1:** the press release attached to the 8-K.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, with one-time items stripped out.
- **R4 at the ask:** the lowest Buy target must sit above strike + the price we'd actually pay (the ask).
