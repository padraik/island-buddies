## GLOSSARY

- **Event-dated:** a target whose date is pinned by what it reacted to (e.g. "after earnings miss" = after the Oct 29 print) rather than by a printed calendar date.
- **R1-R7:** binder Tab 1 Iron Rules. R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 ratings mix, R4 bear floor (lowest Buy target above breakeven), R5 price cap on reserve, R6 reachability (needed move <= 1.5x median earnings move), R7 liquidity (OI >= 250, spread <= 25% of mid).
- **BE:** breakeven at expiry, strike + premium paid.
- **OI:** open interest, contracts currently open on that strike.
- **6-K filer:** foreign private issuer. No 10-Q deadline, so R2 needs both the tool date and last year's date.
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor counts only if it's a Buy-rated target dated inside 60 days and published after the decline.
- **Neutral / Hold:** not a Buy rating, so it can't be the Rule 4 floor.
- **Ratio:** median earnings move divided by the option's time value as a % of the stock. R6 needs about 0.67 or better at the ask.
- **Ramp sell:** selling into the pre-earnings premium instead of holding through the print.
- **(f) / verified: false:** date estimated from cadence, not announced by the company.
- **ADR:** US-traded share of a foreign company.
