## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** binder Tab 1 Iron Rules. R1 52-week range percentile (calls <= 0.25), R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus (<= 1 Sell, >= 3 Buys, Buys >= Holds), R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 ask x 100 <= tier % of reserve, R6 needed move <= 1.5x median earnings move, R7 OI >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid.
- **R4 proxy:** screening shortcut: tool low target well above the stock (more than ~8%), before any contract is picked.
- **(f) / (v):** earnings date `verified: false` (estimated) / `verified: true` (company-announced).
- **LY:** last year's same-quarter report date.
- **BE:** breakeven at expiry, strike + premium.
- **Floor-gated:** passes R4 on the numbers, but no Buy target is dated after the stock's decline (binder Tab 6), so no entry yet.
- **Slide-in:** a name sitting just above R1's 0.25 line that could fall under it.
- **Paper:** tracked in `paper_book.md` only, no money.
