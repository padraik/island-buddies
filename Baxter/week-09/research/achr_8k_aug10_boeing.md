## What it says

**Consideration, all stock, at closing:**
> "a number of shares ... of Class A common stock ... equal to 19.75% of the shares of the Company's Class A common stock outstanding as of immediately prior to the date of Closing"

plus two warrants, each for $100.0M / the 5-day VWAP before closing, struck at **$13.00** and **$17.88**. Boeing is locked up 12 months on the consideration shares (hedging allowed).

**Built-in equity raise (the part that matters for the offering risk):**
> "the Company may elect, in its sole discretion and on a single occasion, to issue and sell to Boeing, and Boeing will be required to purchase, a number of shares ... having an aggregate purchase price of up to $55.0 million. Any such issuance and sale will be made in connection with an offering of Class A common stock by the Company to third-party investors that is expected to result in gross proceeds to the Company of at least $400.0 million"

Window: before the later of Mar 31, 2027 and three months after closing. Needs stockholder approval. Separately: "if the Company raises additional equity capital under certain conditions, shares issued in such capital raise may be excluded from the calculation of the Consideration Shares", so a raise before closing doesn't inflate Boeing's 19.75%.

**Closing:** HSR (expired, 8-K Sep 24), national-security / foreign-investment approvals, no MAE, NYSE listing of the shares. Outside date May 9, 2027 (+3 months extendable). Boeing can walk if Archer's enterprise value sits below a minimum level for a specified period.

## What it means for the $4C Dec18

- The 19.75% dilution has been public since Aug 10, through a ~$7 to $4.20 slide. Not new to the market.
- A **$400M+ offering is pre-wired** into the deal, with a reason to do it before closing. That makes Bearxter's offering risk concrete, not hypothetical. Last year Archer announced a $650M registered direct on **Nov 6, 2025, its Q3 print day**.
- The doc's exit already sells the ramp Mon Nov 9 3:35pm, before the after-close print, so a print-day raise lands after we're out. The exposure is a raise between entry and Nov 9. Raising $400M at a 52-week low ($4.20) with the deal not closed is possible, not obvious.
- No S-3, 424B or new 8-K since Oct 2 on EDGAR (checked 8:35am ET Nov 2). The doc's ORDER line ("no offering") still passes.
- Verdict: doesn't flip the doc. OPEN's addendum quotes this file and keeps confidence at the bottom of the 3.5/5 tier.

## GLOSSARY
- **8-K:** a filing companies must make within a few days of a material event.
- **Item 1.01:** the 8-K item for signing a material definitive agreement.
- **Consideration shares:** stock issued as the purchase price instead of cash.
- **Warrant:** a right to buy shares at a fixed price for a set period; dilutive if exercised.
- **VWAP:** volume-weighted average price over a period.
- **Lock-up:** a period the holder agrees not to sell.
- **Forward equity purchase:** a pre-agreed commitment for an investor to buy shares later, here tied to a $400M+ public raise.
- **Registered direct offering:** a sale of registered shares straight to chosen investors, fast, usually at a discount.
- **HSR:** the US antitrust waiting period for large deals.
- **S-3 / 424B:** the shelf registration and the prospectus supplement that show an offering is happening.
