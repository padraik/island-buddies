## GLOSSARY

- **R1-R7:** the Iron Rules (binder Tab 1). R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 price cap vs reserve, R6 reachability (move to BE <= 1.5x median print), R7 liquidity (OI >= 250, spread <= 25% of mid).
- **Median print:** the middle value of the absolute earnings-day moves; sign ignored.
- **Ratio (median / TV%):** median print divided by the near-ATM contract's time value as a percent of the stock price. >= 0.67 at the ask is the R6 pass line for an ATM strike.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid.
- **Floor gate:** binder Tab 6: a Rule 4 target only counts if it's dated within 60 days and after the stock's decline. A ratings record with no `updated_at` stamp can't prove that.
- **R4 proxy:** the free screening version of Rule 4: lowest target above today's price.
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding.
- **Band:** a price slice of the scanner (A $1-8.19, B $8.19-25, C $25-100, D $100+), so each scan stays under the 200-row cap.
- **Paper size:** a contract that only the paper book can hold at today's reserve.
- **(f):** the tool's date is `verified: false` (estimated). **LY:** last year's same-quarter report date.
- **LA:** large accelerated filer (10-K due 60 days after year end).
- **Ramp line:** the sell-the-ramp deadline, the trading day before the print.
