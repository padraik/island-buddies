## GLOSSARY

- **R1 (range percentile):** where the stock sits in its 52-week range, 0 = at the low. Calls want under 0.25.
- **R2 (calendar):** the print has to land at least 21 days before expiry (later of the tool date and last year's date), with the 10-Q/10-K deadline at least 5 trading days before expiry (6-K filers: both dates present instead). "Already reported" means the catalyst is spent.
- **R3 (ratings):** 3+ Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds, 0-1 Sells. No coverage fails it.
- **R4 (floor):** breakeven has to sit under the lowest Buy-side price target, dated within 60 days and after the decline (Tab 6). "Proxy" means using the tool's low target against the stock price, before a strike is picked.
- **R5 (price cap):** one contract's ask x 100 can't exceed the conviction tier's percent of reserve ($0.706/share at 3.5/5 tonight).
- **R6 (reachability):** the move to breakeven can't exceed 1.5x the median earnings-day move.
- **R7 (liquidity floor):** open interest of at least 250 and a spread of at most 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **MID / OUT R1:** the stock sits in the middle of its range, so there's no calls edge.
- **OI:** open interest, the contracts currently outstanding.
- **BE:** breakeven, strike plus premium paid.
- **(f):** forecast earnings date, not yet announced by the company.
- **Gate:** a condition written into a research doc that has to clear on live data before any entry.
- **Park:** the name passed what can be checked now; the next check waits on a date (~Dec 15, when spring chains carry OI).
- **AH:** after-hours trading price.
