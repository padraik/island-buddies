## GLOSSARY

- **R1 (range percentile):** where the stock sits in its 52-week range, (price - low) / (high - low). Calls need under 0.25.
- **R2:** a confirmed earnings date before expiry, with a 21-day cushion on the later of the tool date and last year's date.
- **R3 (calls):** at least 3 Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds, at most 1 Sell.
- **R4 proxy:** the lowest analyst target must sit above the stock at screen time; at entry, above breakeven.
- **R5:** the per-contract price cap, 10% of reserve at 3.5/5 conviction ($0.539 tonight).
- **R6:** breakeven must be within 1.5x the stock's median earnings-day move.
- **R7 (liquidity floor):** open interest >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **AH:** after-hours trading. **(v) / (f):** company-verified / estimated earnings date. **OI:** open interest. **BE:** breakeven (strike + premium).
- **CEF:** closed-end fund, a pooled income vehicle with no earnings catalyst.
