## GLOSSARY

- **R1:** (price - 52-week low) / (52-week high - 52-week low). <= 0.25 is the calls band.
- **R2:** expiry at least 21 days after the later of the tool date and last year's date, and the 10-Q deadline at least 5 trading days before expiry (foreign filers: both dates required, no deadline test).
- **R3 (calls):** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, and Buys at least equal to Holds.
- **R4 (Bear Floor):** lowest Buy target above the call's breakeven (strike + premium). "R4 proxy" kill: the lowest target is already at or under the stock price, so no call can pass.
- **R5:** one contract's ask x 100 must fit the conviction tier's share of reserve (3.5/5 = 10% of $539.11 = $53.91, so an ask of $0.539 or less).
- **R6 (Reachability):** the move needed to reach breakeven is no more than 1.5x the median earnings-day move over the last 4-8 prints.
- **R7 (Liquidity Floor):** open interest >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **B/H/S:** Buy / Hold / Sell rating counts from `get_equity_analyst_ratings`.
- **BE:** breakeven, strike + premium paid.
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding.
- **(v) / (f):** the earnings date is company-verified / estimated from cadence.
- **LY:** last year's same-quarter report date.
- **Closing book:** the 3:59pm quote, often wider than the daytime market; a Monday live quote can differ.
