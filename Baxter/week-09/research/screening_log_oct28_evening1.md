## GLOSSARY

- **Range pct / R1:** (price - 52w low) / (52w high - 52w low). Under 0.25 = calls zone. 0.25-0.74 = MID-OUT.
- **R2:** the earnings print is proven to land before expiry (21 days after the later of the tool date and last year's date; 10-Q deadline 5+ trading days before expiry). "Spent" = it prints before we could hold and sell the ramp.
- **R3:** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys >= Holds.
- **R4 / R4 proxy:** the lowest Buy target must be above breakeven (strike + premium). Proxy at the free screen: the tool's low target vs today's price.
- **R5:** ask x 100 <= 10% of reserve (3.5/5 tier). Reserve = settled spendable cash.
- **R6:** the move to breakeven <= 1.5x the median earnings-day move (last 4-8 prints).
- **R7:** open interest >= 250 and (ask - bid) / mid <= 25%, on the exact contract.
- **BE:** breakeven, strike + premium paid.
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding.
- **Slide-in scan:** names just above the R1 line (0.25-0.29) with a print in the window, watched for crossing under.
- **MID-OUT:** middle of the 52-week range, no direction, no further work.
