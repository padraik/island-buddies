## GLOSSARY

- **Band:** a price slice of the scan (A $1-8.19, B $8.19-25, C $25-100, D $100+).
- **R1 / Range pct:** where the price sits in its 52-week range; under 0.25 is the calls pool. Negative values mean the all-day price is under the weekly-bar low.
- **R3:** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds.
- **R4 proxy:** lowest analyst target above today's price. The real Rule 4 test is lowest Buy target above the call's breakeven, checked on the chain.
- **(f):** `verified: false`, the tool's estimated date, not company-announced.
- **LA:** large accelerated filer (10-K due 60 days after year end).
- **6-K filer:** foreign private issuer; no 10-Q deadline, so Rule 2 needs both the tool date and last year's date.
- **Park:** passed the free checks; waits for the next funnel stage.
- **Ratio:** median earnings-day move / (near-ATM time value as % of stock). Rule 6 needs about 0.67 or better at the ask.
