## GLOSSARY

- **Date-staging:** working out which option expiry a name's next earnings date allows under Rule 2, before spending a chain check.
- **Listed expiry:** an expiration that actually appears on the stock's option chain.
- **R1 / Rule 1:** the stock sits in the bottom 20-25% of its 52-week range: (price - low) / (high - low).
- **R2 / Rule 2:** the expiry must be at least 21 days after the later of the tool date and last year's date. US filers also need the filing deadline at least 5 trading days before expiry.
- **Large accelerated filer (LA):** $700M+ public float on the last day of Q2. Files its 10-K within 60 days (Mar 1). Accelerated: 75 days (Mar 16).
- **R3:** at least 3 Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds, at most 1 Sell.
- **R4 proxy:** at screening, is the lowest analyst target above the stock price?
- **R5 line:** the most one contract can cost: 10% of spendable cash at 3.5/5, so $0.716 a share tonight.
- **R6 / Rule 6:** the move to breakeven can't be more than 1.5x the stock's median earnings move.
- **R7 / Rule 7:** open interest at least 250 and spread at most 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **Floor gate / ratings stamp:** the lowest Buy target has to be dated after the stock's slide to count as a floor. The stamp is the `updated_at` on the ratings record.
- **20-F / 6-K / 40-F:** forms foreign companies file with the SEC instead of 10-Ks and 10-Qs.
- **(f):** `verified: false`, an estimated date.
- **Ramp sell / ramp line:** selling before the print into the pre-earnings premium; the line is the last run to do it.
- **Paper book:** tracked trades with no money, used to test the rules on names the real fund can't afford.
- **AH:** after-hours. **OI:** open interest. **BE:** breakeven (strike + premium).
- **Weeklies:** weekly expirations, a sign the options trade actively.
