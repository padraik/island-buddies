## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** the binder's Iron Rules (Tab 1). R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 affordability, R6 reachability, R7 liquidity floor.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid ($12.50 + $2.25 = $14.75).
- **ITM (in the money):** a call whose strike is under the stock price; part of its price is intrinsic value ($13.46 - $12.50 = $0.96).
- **PM print:** results after the close; the move is measured print-day close to next-day close, and the ramp sell is the print day's last run.
- **Dated floor / stale floor:** Tab 6 counts a Buy target as a floor only if it's dated within 60 days and was published after the decline.
- **Driver bucket:** the main macro force behind a stock (here, the precious-metals price). Tab 3 caps the real fund at 35% of reserve per bucket; whether the paper book follows that is an open proposal.
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print into the pre-earnings premium (Tab 4).
- **IV, theta, delta, OI:** implied volatility; daily decay; option move per $1 of stock; contracts outstanding.
- **Paper book:** tracked-only positions, entered at the ask and exited at the bid, no money.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Prime (decision), Bullxter (for), Bearxter (against), Calxter (math), Macxter (context).
