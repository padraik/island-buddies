## GLOSSARY

- **R1 (Rule 1):** the stock sits in the bottom 20-25% of its 52-week range. Calculated here as (price - 52-week low) / (52-week high - 52-week low).
- **R2 (Rule 2):** a dated earnings report has to land before expiry, with 21+ days of cushion after the later of the tool date and last year's date.
- **R4 (Rule 4, bear floor):** the lowest analyst Buy target has to sit above our breakeven.
- **R5 (Rule 5):** one contract's ask can't exceed the tier line, 10% of reserve at 3.5/5 ($0.716/share tonight), and never above $1.00/share until the unlock.
- **R6 (Rule 6, reachability):** the move needed to reach breakeven can't exceed 1.5x the stock's median earnings-day move.
- **R6 structural:** the median print is so small that no spring contract can pass, so no quote is pulled.
- **R7 (Rule 7, liquidity):** open interest at least 250 and a bid/ask spread no wider than 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **Ratio:** median print divided by near-the-money time value as a percent of the stock. Under ~0.67 at the ask, R6 can't pass.
- **Floor gate / stamp:** the lowest Buy target has to be dated after the stock's slide. With no `updated_at` stamp on the ratings record, the floor isn't trusted.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike plus premium paid.
- **OI (open interest):** contracts outstanding on that strike and expiry.
- **(f) / (v):** earnings date estimated from cadence / verified by the company.
- **6-K / 40-F filer:** foreign issuers who don't file a 10-Q, so there's no 10-Q deadline test, but both dates are required.
- **Keep Watching / Stopped Watching:** passes.md outcomes. Stopped means a written trigger fired.
