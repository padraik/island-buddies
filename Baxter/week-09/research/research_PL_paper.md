## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** the binder's Iron Rules (Tab 1). R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 affordability, R6 reachability, R7 liquidity floor.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid ($12 + $2.90 = $14.90).
- **ITM (in the money):** a call whose strike is under the stock price; part of its price is intrinsic value, what's left is extrinsic (time) value.
- **PM print:** results after the close; the move is measured from that day's close to the next day's close.
- **Delta / theta / vega:** option move per $1 of stock / daily decay / move per 1 point of implied volatility.
- **IV (implied volatility):** the move the option market is pricing; it usually rises into a print and collapses after it.
- **ATM program:** at-the-market offering; the company sells new shares into the market over time.
- **10-Q deadline:** a large accelerated filer has 40 days after quarter end to file its quarterly report.
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor counts only if the Buy target is dated within 60 days and published after the stock's decline.
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print into the pre-earnings premium (Tab 4).
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding. **Paper book:** tracked-only positions, entered at the ask and exited at the bid, no money.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Prime (decision), Bullxter (for), Bearxter (against), Calxter (math), Macxter (context).
