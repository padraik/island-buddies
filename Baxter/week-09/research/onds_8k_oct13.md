## GLOSSARY

- **8-K:** a filing companies must make within a few days of a material event.
- **Item 7.01 / Regulation FD:** a disclosure made publicly so no investor hears it first.
- **Item 8.01:** "other events" the company chooses to report.
- **Item 3.02:** unregistered sale of equity securities, i.e. new shares issued (dilution).
- **EX-99:** an exhibit attached to the filing, usually the press release.
- **Rule 6 (Reachability):** the move to breakeven must be no more than 1.5x the stock's median earnings-reaction move.
