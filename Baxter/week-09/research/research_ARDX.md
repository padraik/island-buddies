## GLOSSARY

- **Range percentile:** where the stock sits between its 52-week low (0) and high (1). Under 0.25 = calls zone.
- **In the money (ITM):** for a call, the stock is above the strike. This $3 call with the stock at $3.265 already has $0.265 of real (intrinsic) value.
- **Intrinsic value / time value:** intrinsic is what the option would be worth if exercised right now (stock minus strike). Everything paid above that is time value, which decays.
- **Breakeven (BE):** strike + premium paid.
- **Bear floor / Rule 4:** the lowest analyst price target must sit above our breakeven; if it drops below, we sell that day.
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor only counts if it was set within 60 days and after the stock's drop; older targets are a countdown to a downgrade.
- **Rule 5:** one contract's ask can't exceed the conviction tier's top percent of reserve (10% at 3.5/5).
- **Rule 6 (reachability):** the move needed to breakeven must be no more than 1.5x the stock's median earnings-day move over its last 4-8 prints.
- **Large accelerated filer:** an SEC category for companies with $700M+ public float. It has to file its 10-Q within 40 days of quarter end (Nov 9 for the Sep 30 quarter).
- **PM print:** the company reports after the 4pm close; the reaction shows up the next trading day.
- **Verified (earnings date):** the company announced the date itself, rather than a data vendor estimating it.
- **IV ramp / sell the ramp:** option prices tend to rise into earnings; we sell into that rise instead of holding through the report.
- **Open interest (OI):** contracts outstanding on that strike; a rough liquidity gauge. 50 is thin.
- **Spread:** the gap between bid and ask. Here it's $0.40 on a $0.35 mid.
- **GTC:** good-til-cancelled; a resting order that stays live across days.
- **Prior authorization / step edit:** insurer rules that make a patient get approval, or try a cheaper drug first, before the insurer pays for this one. They slow new prescriptions.
- **IBSRELA / XPHOZAH:** Ardelyx's two marketed drugs (both tenapanor): IBS with constipation, and phosphate control in dialysis.
- **Class action:** a shareholder lawsuit, usually filed by plaintiff firms after a big drop.
- **Estimate revisions:** whether analysts have been raising or lowering their EPS forecasts recently.
