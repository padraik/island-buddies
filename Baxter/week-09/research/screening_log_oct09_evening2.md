## GLOSSARY
- **R1 / range pct:** where the stock sits in its 52-week range; < 0.25 = calls side.
- **R2:** earnings proven to land before expiry: expiry >= 21 days after the later of the tool date and last year's date; 10-Q deadline >= 5 trading days before expiry (US filers only).
- **R3:** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys >= Holds.
- **R4 proxy:** the tool's lowest target vs price; a real R4 needs the lowest **Buy-rated** target, dated within 60 days and after the decline, above breakeven.
- **R6:** move to breakeven <= 1.5x median earnings-day move (last six prints).
- **R7:** OI >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **(f):** earnings date not company-verified.
- **6-K / 40-F filer:** foreign company that doesn't file a US 10-Q, so the 10-Q deadline test doesn't apply and both dates are required.
- **Equal Weight / Market Perform / Hold:** neutral ratings; their targets are never a floor.
