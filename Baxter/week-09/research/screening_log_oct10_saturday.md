## GLOSSARY
- **R1 / range pct:** where the stock sits in its 52-week range; < 0.25 = calls side.
- **R2:** earnings proven to land before expiry: expiry >= 21 days after the later of the tool date and last year's date; 10-Q deadline >= 5 trading days before expiry (US filers only).
- **R3:** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys >= Holds.
- **R4:** the lowest **Buy-rated** target, dated within 60 days and after the decline, must sit above breakeven. The tool's `low_price_target` is the lowest of any rating (and sometimes years old), so it gets dated by name.
- **R4 proxy:** the free screen version: tool low above the stock price. A kill here can be reopened if the low turns out to be a Hold's or a Sell's.
- **R5:** one contract's ask x 100 must fit the conviction tier's percentage of reserve (3.5/5 = 10% = $67.63 today).
- **R6:** move to breakeven <= 1.5x the median earnings-day move (last six prints).
- **R7:** OI >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid.
- **(f):** earnings date not company-verified.
- **6-K filer:** foreign company that doesn't file a US 10-Q.
- **OI (open interest):** contracts outstanding on that strike; updates once a day, overnight.
- **Paper book:** tracked-only positions in names too expensive for the real fund, entered at the ask and exited at the bid.
