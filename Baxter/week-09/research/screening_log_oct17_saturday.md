## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** binder Tab 1 Iron Rules: R1 52-week range percentile <= 0.25, R2 earnings proven before expiry, R3 analyst consensus (<= 1 Sell, >= 3 Buys, Buys >= Holds), R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 affordability (dropped for paper), R6 breakeven move <= 1.5x the median earnings move, R7 OI >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid.
- **B/H/S:** Buy / Hold / Sell rating counts from `get_equity_analyst_ratings`.
- **(v) / (f):** earnings date verified by the company / forecast from cadence. **LY:** same quarter last year.
- **BE / needs:** strike + ask, and the stock move needed to reach it.
- **Median / cap:** median absolute earnings-reaction move over the last 4 prints, and 1.5x that (R6's limit).
- **Floor gate:** a name that passes everything except proof that its lowest Buy target is dated after the decline (Tab 6).
- **Paper:** tracked in `paper_book.md`, no money; R5 dropped.
- **Preview scan:** a scanner query run without saving it.
