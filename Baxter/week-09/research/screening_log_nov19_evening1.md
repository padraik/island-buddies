## GLOSSARY

- **R1 (range percentile):** (price - 52-week low) / (52-week high - 52-week low). Under 0.25 = calls candidate. Negative = closed under the scanner's recorded 52-week low (new low today).
- **R2:** the earnings print is proven to land before expiry: expiry 21+ days after the later of the tool date and last year's date, and the 10-K deadline 5+ trading days before expiry (40-F / 20-F filers: both dates required, no deadline test).
- **R3 (calls):** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds.
- **R4 proxy:** at screening, the lowest analyst target must be above today's price. The real Rule 4 test (lowest Buy target above strike + premium) happens on the chain.
- **R5:** one contract's ask x 100 must fit the conviction tier on reserve (3.5/5 = 10% = $71.60 tonight).
- **R6:** the move to breakeven must be no more than 1.5x the median earnings-day move over the last 4-8 prints.
- **R7:** the exact contract needs open interest >= 250 and a spread <= 25% of mid.
- **LA / large accelerated filer:** a US company with $700M+ public float; its 10-K is due 60 days after year-end (Mar 1), the only deadline early enough for a Mar19 expiry.
- **Park / stage:** park = passed the free ratings screen; staged = has a dated R2 verdict and a listed expiry, waiting for the spring chain.
- **Floor gate / stamp:** binder Tab 6: a Rule 4 target only counts if it's dated within 60 days and after the decline. A 2019 stamp means the floor can't be verified from the tool.
