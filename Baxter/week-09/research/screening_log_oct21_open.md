## GLOSSARY

- **R1-R7:** the Iron Rules in binder Tab 1. R1 range position, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 bear floor (lowest Buy target above breakeven), R5 price cap, R6 reachability (needed move vs 1.5x median print move), R7 liquidity (OI >= 250, spread <= 25% of mid).
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid.
- **R6 cap:** 1.5x the median absolute earnings-reaction move. AM prints measured prior close -> print-day close; PM prints print-day close -> next close.
- **R6 line:** the highest ask that still passes Rule 6 at a given stock price.
- **Opening quote:** the first minutes after 9:30, when option spreads are wide and bids are unreliable.
- **Paper-size:** a contract whose one-lot price is over the real fund's Rule 5 line; it can only go in the paper book.
- **Structural R6 kill:** the stock's median print move is so small that no affordable strike can reach breakeven.
- **Floor-dating gate (Tab 6):** a bear floor only counts if it was published within 60 days and after the decline.
