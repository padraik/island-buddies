**What the deal is:** Alcoa is the **buyer**. The 425s cover "Alcoa's proposed transaction to acquire South32 Limited's equity interests in certain bauxite, alumina, and aluminum assets" (called AliGroup). It was announced Jun 30, 2026, and "South32 shareholders approved the transaction on October 15".
- **Consideration:** "cash consideration of approximately $3.1 billion", funded by $2.6B of new notes. Also a CVR, "Maximum of $750 million". The pro forma share count (282.8M vs 265.8M) suggests about 17M new shares. That's my arithmetic; these filings don't state an exchange ratio.
- **Timing:** "The transaction remains on track to close in the first half of 2027."
- **Q3:** reported Oct 15 PM. Adj EPS $1.82 vs $1.52 est. Production and shipment guidance reaffirmed. Interest expense raised to ~$185M because of the notes.

**For the screen:** binder M&A flag (calls) caps upside only when the company is the *target* at a deal price. Alcoa is the acquirer, so there's no cap and no R4 substitute. Dilution and new leverage are real Bearxter points, but they're not a rule. The name died anyway on R6 at the Mar19 chain (see `screening_log_oct20_midday1035.md`).

## GLOSSARY
- **8-K:** a current report a company files for material events (here, the earnings release).
- **Rule 425:** filings of communications about a business combination.
- **CVR:** contingent value right, an extra payment to the seller that depends on future conditions.
- **R4 / R6:** Iron Rules 4 (bear floor) and 6 (reachability), binder Tab 1.
