## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** the binder's Iron Rules (Tab 1). R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 affordability, R6 reachability, R7 liquidity floor.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid ($160 + $5.20 = $165.20).
- **Median print move:** the middle of the last six earnings-day moves, ignoring direction.
- **Spectrum:** licensed radio frequencies a carrier uses to run its network; 800 MHz is "low-band," good for coverage over long distances and indoors.
- **Stale floor:** an analyst target published before the drop it's supposed to protect against (binder Tab 6, Jul 10).
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print into the pre-earnings premium (Tab 4).
- **IV (implied volatility):** the move the option price assumes, annualized; rises into earnings, collapses after.
- **Theta:** what the option loses per day if nothing else changes.
- **OI (open interest):** contracts outstanding on that strike.
- **Paper book:** tracked-only positions, entered at the ask and exited at the bid, no money.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Prime (decision), Bullxter (for), Bearxter (against), Calxter (math), Macxter (context).
