## GLOSSARY

- **Date-staging:** working out which option expiry month a name's next earnings date allows under Rule 2, before spending a chain check.
- **R2 / Rule 2:** the earnings date must land at least 21 days before expiry (the later of the tool date and last year's date), and the filing deadline at least 5 trading days before expiry.
- **R3:** at least 3 Buys, Buys at least equal to Holds, at most 1 Sell.
- **R4 proxy:** at screening, is the lowest analyst target above the stock price? (The real Rule 4 compares it to breakeven at entry.)
- **Floor gate:** the lowest Buy target has to be dated after the stock's slide, or it doesn't count as a floor.
- **(f):** `verified: false`, an estimated date, not company-announced.
- **OI:** open interest, the number of contracts outstanding.
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print into the pre-earnings premium.
- **Park:** passes the free screens, waiting on a later check.
- **10-K deadline:** 60 / 75 / 90 days after fiscal year-end for large accelerated / accelerated / non-accelerated filers (Mar 1 / Mar 16 / Mar 31 for a calendar year).
