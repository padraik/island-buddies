## What it is

Item 5.07 (Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders). Annual meeting held Thu Oct 30. This is the "vote 8-K due Thu Nov 5" that positions.md and the watch file were waiting for. No exhibits.

## The key sentences, verbatim

> "Although each nominee received a majority of the votes cast, neither nominee received the affirmative vote of a majority of the shares outstanding and entitled to vote, as required by Section 2.8 of the Company's Amended and Restated Bylaws... As a result, neither nominee was elected..."

> "Accordingly, Governor Perry and Mr. Stein each continue to serve as Class I directors of the Company, with the same authority as any other director, and there is no vacancy on the Board of Directors."

> "Based on information available to the Company, the Company believes that those investors' shares were instead voted against Governor Perry, in breach of the DNA. Those investors, through counsel, have disputed that the DNA required them to vote for Governor Perry."

> "The Company reserves all rights and remedies available to it under the DNA, at law, and in equity, including the right to seek an order giving effect to the votes the DNA required."

## GLOSSARY

- **8-K:** a filing companies must make within a few days of a material event.
- **Item 5.07:** the 8-K item that reports shareholder-vote results.
- **Form 144:** notice an insider or affiliate files when planning to sell restricted or control stock. It's a plan to sell, not proof of a sale.
- **DNA (Director Nomination Agreement):** a contract giving certain investors the right to nominate directors, and here obliging them to vote for each other's nominees.
- **Broker non-votes:** shares held by brokers that didn't get voting instructions; they count toward "outstanding" but not "cast."
- **Class I director:** one of three staggered board classes; Class I's term would have run to the 2029 meeting.
- **Rule 4:** the binder's analyst floor: the lowest Buy-rated target must stay above our breakeven.
