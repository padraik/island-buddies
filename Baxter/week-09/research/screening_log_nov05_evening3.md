## GLOSSARY

- **R1:** (price - 52-week low) / (52-week high - 52-week low). <= 0.25 is the calls band; this scan took < 0.30 to catch slide-ins.
- **R2:** expiry at least 21 days after the later of the tool date and last year's date, and the 10-Q deadline at least 5 trading days before expiry (6-K filers: both dates required, no deadline test).
- **R3 (calls):** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, and Buys at least equal to Holds.
- **B/H/S:** Buy / Hold / Sell rating counts from `get_equity_analyst_ratings`.
- **R4:** lowest Buy target above breakeven, with the target dated after the stock's decline.
- **R5:** one contract's ask x 100 at or under the tier cap on reserve (real money only; paper skips it).
- **R6:** move to breakeven <= 1.5x the median absolute earnings reaction (last 4-8 prints).
- **R7:** open interest >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **BE:** breakeven, strike plus premium (here at the mid).
- **(f):** date estimated from cadence (`verified: false`).
- **OI:** open interest, the number of contracts outstanding.
- **Slide-in:** a name just above the R1 line that could fall into the band.
