## GLOSSARY

- **R1-R7:** binder Tab 1 Iron Rules. R1 range percentile, R2 confirmed catalyst before expiry, R3 rating consensus, R4 bear floor, R5 price cap on reserve, R6 reachability (needed move <= 1.5x median earnings move), R7 liquidity (OI >= 250, spread <= 25% of mid).
- **BE:** breakeven at expiry, strike + premium.
- **ITM:** in the money; the strike is below the stock price.
- **Delta:** how much the option moves per $1 in the stock (0.60 = about 60 cents).
- **Theta:** time decay per day, in dollars per share.
- **IV:** implied volatility, the option market's priced-in move.
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor counts only if dated inside 60 days and published after the decline.
- **Paper:** tracked in `paper_book.md`, no money moves; fills at the ask, exits at the bid.
- **Ramp sell:** selling into the pre-earnings premium instead of holding through the print.
- **10-K backstop:** the SEC annual-report deadline (60 days after fiscal year end for large accelerated filers); stands in for the 10-Q deadline when the print is a fiscal Q4.
- **Split-adjusted:** prices restated for the April 2026 3-for-1 split so old and new prices compare.
- **Switchgear / e-house:** the electrical gear that distributes high-voltage power inside plants and data centers; Powell's core product.
- **AI-capex bucket:** paper positions whose main driver is hyperscaler data-center spending (AVGO, GOOGL, and POWL if entered).
