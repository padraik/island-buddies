## GLOSSARY

- **Paper:** tracked in `paper_book.md`, no money moves; fills at the ask, exits at the bid.

- **Ramp sell:** selling into the pre-earnings premium instead of holding through the print.
- **(f) / `verified: false`:** date estimated from cadence, not announced.
- **ASIC:** application-specific integrated circuit, the custom AI chips Marvell designs for hyperscalers.
