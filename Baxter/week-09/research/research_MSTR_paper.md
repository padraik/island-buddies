## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** the binder's Iron Rules (Tab 1). R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 affordability, R6 reachability, R7 liquidity floor.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid ($120 + $22.05 = $142.05).
- **52-week window rolling off:** the range in R1 covers the last 52 weeks. When the old high falls out of the window, the high drops, and so does the 25% line, even if the stock doesn't move.
- **In the money (ITM):** a call whose strike is under the stock price. Intrinsic value is stock minus strike ($16.02 here); extrinsic (time) value is the rest ($6.03), and that's the part that decays.
- **Delta:** how much the option moves per $1 of stock (0.75 here). **Theta:** daily decay (-$0.159). **IV:** implied volatility (75%).
- **Bitcoin proxy:** a stock whose price mostly follows bitcoin because the company holds a lot of it.
- **Mark-to-market:** the quarter's profit or loss includes the change in bitcoin's price over the quarter, which is known before the report.
- **PM print:** results after the close; the move is measured print-day close to next-day close, and the ramp sell is the print day's last run.
- **10-Q deadline:** a large accelerated filer has 40 days after quarter end to file its quarterly report (Sep 30 -> Nov 9).
- **Stale floor:** an analyst target published before the drop it's supposed to protect against (binder Tab 6).
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print into the pre-earnings premium (Tab 4).
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding. **Paper book:** tracked-only positions, entered at the ask and exited at the bid, no money.
- **BOTZ problem:** a theme without a dated mechanism; named for Sheldon's robotics ETF.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Prime (decision), Bullxter (for), Bearxter (against), Calxter (math), Macxter (context).
