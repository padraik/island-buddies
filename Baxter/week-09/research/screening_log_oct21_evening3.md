## GLOSSARY

- **R1-R7:** the Iron Rules (binder Tab 1): range percentile, earnings before expiry, ratings, bear floor, chain filter, reachability, liquidity.
- **R4 proxy:** the free screen version of Rule 4: if the lowest analyst target is at or under the stock price, no call breakeven can sit under it.
- **R6 structural:** the median earnings move is so small that the premium alone on the first legal expiry exceeds the 1.5x cap, so no strike can pass.
- **R6 cap:** 1.5x the median absolute earnings-day move over the last six or seven prints.
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor counts only if the Buy target is dated within 60 days and after the stock's slide.
- **Stamp:** the `updated_at` date on the tool's analyst-target data.
- **(v) / (f):** tool date verified / not verified by the company.
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding.
- **Paper (gated):** a paper-size name that has passed the free rules and waits on named checks before a doc and a paper entry.
