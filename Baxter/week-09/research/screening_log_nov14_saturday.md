## GLOSSARY

- **R1 (range percentile):** (price - 52-week low) / (52-week high - 52-week low). Under 0.25 = calls candidate.
- **R2:** the earnings print is proven to land before expiry: expiry 21+ days after the later of the tool date and last year's date, and the 10-Q / 10-K deadline 5+ trading days before expiry (6-K filers: both dates required, no deadline test).
- **R3:** at most 1 Sell, at least 3 Buys, Buys >= Holds. No coverage counts as a fail.
- **R4 proxy / R4:** the lowest analyst target must sit above the stock (screen) and above the call's breakeven (rule).
- **R5:** one contract's ask x 100 fits the conviction tier's share of reserve ($0.716/share at 3.5/5).
- **R6:** the breakeven move is no larger than 1.5x the median absolute earnings reaction over the last 4-8 prints.
- **R7:** open interest >= 250 and spread <= 25% of mid on the exact contract.
- **Floor gate:** Tab 6 rule. A Rule 4 floor counts only if the target is dated within 60 days and after the stock's decline.
- **Band:** a price slice of the December re-sweep scan (A $1-8.19, B $8.19-25, C $25-100, D $100+, E under $1).
- **Park:** passes the free screen, but the chain can't be judged yet, so the name waits for the ~Dec 15 run.
- **Cushion:** how far the lowest analyst target sits above Friday's close, in percent.
- **6-K filer:** foreign private issuer (NIO), no 10-Q deadline.
