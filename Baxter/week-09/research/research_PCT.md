## GLOSSARY

- **Range percentile:** where the stock sits between its 52-week low (0) and high (1). Under 0.25 = calls zone.
- **Breakeven (BE):** strike + premium paid.
- **Bear floor / Rule 4:** the lowest analyst Buy target must sit above our breakeven. Since Jul 10 the target also has to be recent (60 days) and set after the stock fell, or it doesn't count.
- **Stale floor:** a price target set before the stock's decline. It usually gets cut once the analyst updates, which is how CCL, NKE and BSX lost money.
- **Rule 5:** one contract's ask can't exceed the conviction tier's top percent of reserve (10% at 3.5/5).
- **Rule 6 (reachability):** the move to breakeven must be at most 1.5x the median earnings-day move over the last 4-8 prints.
- **PM print:** results after the 4pm close; the reaction is the next day.
- **Verified (earnings date):** announced by the company, not estimated by a data vendor.
- **Converts (convertible notes):** debt that can turn into stock; issuing them and new stock dilutes existing holders.
- **Warrants:** rights to buy stock at a set price; worthless to exercise when the stock is below that price.
- **IV ramp / sell the ramp:** option prices rise into earnings; we sell before the report instead of holding through it.
- **Quant grade (Weiss):** a model-generated rating, not an analyst with a target.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Bullxter (for), Calxter (math), Bearxter (against), Macxter (context), Prime (decision).
