## GLOSSARY
- **R1-R7:** the binder's Iron Rules (Tab 1). R1 range percentile, R2 earnings before expiry, R3 analyst consensus, R4 lowest Buy target above breakeven, R5 affordability, R6 reachability, R7 liquidity floor (OI >= 250, spread <= 25% of mid).
- **Range percentile (R1):** (price - 52-week low) / (52-week high - 52-week low). Under 0.25 is a calls candidate; 0.25-0.75 is MID-OUT.
- **BE (breakeven):** strike + premium paid at the ask.
- **OI:** open interest, contracts outstanding. **Spread:** ask minus bid, as a share of the mid.
- **Median print / cap / line:** median absolute post-earnings move over the last six prints; R6 allows a breakeven move up to 1.5x that; the line is the stock price times (1 + cap).
- **Tab 6 dating:** a floor counts only if the Buy target is dated within 60 days and published after the decline.
- **Jan15 / Feb19 / Mar19:** standard monthly option expiries (third Friday). Dec18 is also a monthly, but on some names it's thinly traded.
- **Ramp sell:** selling before the print, into the pre-earnings premium (Tab 4).
- **Paper book:** tracked-only positions, entered at the ask and exited at the bid, no money.
- **(v) / verified:** `get_earnings_results` flag: company-announced vs estimated.
- **6-K filer:** a foreign private issuer; no 10-Q deadline, so R2 needs both the tool date and last year's date.
