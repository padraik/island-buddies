## GLOSSARY

- **ADR:** American Depositary Receipt, a US-listed share of a foreign company.
- **Range percentile (R1):** where the stock sits between its 52-week low (0) and high (1).
- **Breakeven (BE):** strike + premium paid.
- **Bear floor / Rule 4:** lowest analyst target must stay above breakeven; dated within 60 days and set after the decline (binder Tab 6). Below breakeven = sell that day.
- **Rule 5:** one contract's ask can't exceed the tier's top percent of reserve (10% at 3.5/5), capped at $1.00 for now.
- **Rule 6 (reachability):** move to breakeven <= 1.5x the median earnings-day move over the last 4-8 prints.
- **AM print:** results before the open; the last chance to sell before it is the prior afternoon.
- **Theta / vega:** daily time decay / gain per one-point rise in implied volatility.
- **Delta:** how much the option moves per $1 in the stock (0.52 here: about 52 cents per dollar, per share).
- **IV ramp / sell the ramp:** implied volatility rises into earnings; we sell into that rise, before the print.
- **Ximalaya:** Chinese audio/podcast platform TME acquired in May 2026.
- **Social entertainment:** TME's live-streaming/karaoke segment, the shrinking one.
- **Q3 previews:** analyst notes published in the weeks before earnings, usually with updated targets.
- **Five-Baxter meeting:** Bullxter (for), Calxter (math), Bearxter (against), Macxter (context), Prime (decision).
